# IT265 Module 2: Concept Pitch Cards

## Pitch Card

**Working title: Golfed**

**Player role and situation: Each player gets 1 "brick/piece," and they get to place it on a course. They then have to put/hit a ball from a designated starting position on the course/level. This course would be slightly tilted to slow the player's ball down to prevent them from reaching a golf hole, which is where the ball needs to eventually get to to win. The player who goes next then rolls a ball through that same course with the previous balls still on it. They would then need to get it in the hole, but with the new obstacle put down by the other players. Theoretically, you could block off the hole, but no one would win, and there would be safeguards to prevent the player from putting it close (maybe a foot minimum distance using line of sight from the hole). The win condition would be getting the ball in the hole in the least amount of hits. There would be random maps, and this would be a very mechanics-based game.**

**Repeated decision or action: hitting the ball into the hole and placing bricks/pieces to block the player**

**Goal, pressure, or ending: The goal is to get the lowest score by the end of the gae, and the pressure would be put on by the other players also trying their hardest to win and trip you up.**

**Hook: While the main goal is to have players enjoy the game from the randomness/slight skill required to play, it will ultimately boil down to the players and how they use the pieces they're given; every play session would end up being different.**

**Smallest useful physical prototype: The simplest prototype would involve a ball, a 3d printed course, some papers to keep scores, and a random assortment of obstacles.**

**Question I want listeners to answer: When players play my game, I want them to see how easily they can win while also not messing up themselves when blocking other players.**
